Pennsylvania payroll quick facts (2026)

Pennsylvania's flat 3.07% state rate is the lowest of any state with a flat tax. What actually varies is local: nearly 3,000 municipalities and school districts layer their own Earned Income Tax and a flat Local Services Tax on top, and — unusually — Pennsylvania employees also owe a small unemployment compensation withholding that most other states don't charge workers directly.
