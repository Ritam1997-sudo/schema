Pennsylvania payroll quick facts

Pennsylvania is one of just a handful of states with a flat income tax, and at 3.07% it's the lowest flat rate in the country. Where PA gets more complex is locally: nearly 3,000 municipalities and school districts layer their own Earned Income Tax on top, so two people earning the same salary a few miles apart can take home noticeably different amounts.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Pennsylvania state income tax rate in 2026?

Pennsylvania levies a flat state income tax rate of 3.07% on earned income, regardless of how much you make or your filing status.

Do I have to pay local income tax in Pennsylvania?

Most PA municipalities and school districts levy a local Earned Income Tax (EIT), typically between 1% and 3.75% depending on where you live and work.

Does Pennsylvania tax 401(k) contributions?

Yes. Unlike the federal government, Pennsylvania taxes traditional retirement plan contributions when they're made, but withdrawals in retirement are not taxed.

How much is FICA tax on a Pennsylvania paycheck?

FICA is 7.65% total: 6.2% for Social Security (up to the annual wage base) and 1.45% for Medicare, matched by your employer.

Is this Pennsylvania paycheck calculator exact?

It's a close estimate built from published federal and PA tax figures. Your exact withholding depends on your employer's payroll system, your W-4, and your local tax forms.
