Frequently asked questions.

What is an LED hoarding?

An LED hoarding is an outdoor advertising structure that uses LED modules instead of printed vinyl or flex to display content. It allows dynamic, updatable creative rather than a single static design.

What's the difference between an LED hoarding and a digital billboard?

The terms are often used interchangeably. In practice, "digital billboard" more often refers to full-motion, content-managed displays running multiple rotating campaigns, while "LED hoarding" can refer to any LED-based outdoor structure, static or dynamic.

How much does an LED hoarding cost in India?

Cost depends on display size, pixel pitch, structure type, location and ongoing content/AMC requirements. We recommend a site survey and consultation to provide an accurate project estimate rather than a generic figure.

What pixel pitch should I choose?

Pixel pitch should match expected viewing distance — wider pitch for highway and long-distance viewing, finer pitch for close-range indoor or retail displays. Our team helps select the right pitch during design.

Can LED billboards be controlled remotely?

Yes. Most digital billboards use a centralized content management system, allowing creative to be scheduled, updated or swapped remotely without physical reprinting.

Are outdoor LED displays weather resistant?

Outdoor LED hoardings are built with weatherproof enclosures rated for rain, heat and dust exposure. Protection ratings and build specifications are confirmed per project and location.

Do you provide installation and AMC?

Yes — Riot Color India offers end-to-end service from design and production through installation, with ongoing Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) options for long-term display upkeep.

Can LED displays be rented for events?

Yes. Modular LED wall rentals are available for exhibitions, trade shows and short-duration activations, in addition to permanent hoarding and billboard installations.
