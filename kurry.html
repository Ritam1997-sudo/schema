🌱 our story

Named for the leaf that starts every pot

Karry Palla began with one habit worth keeping: toast the curry leaf first, let the whole kitchen smell like it, then build the rest of the dish around that. Everything on our table — from the slow-dum biryani to the Sunday paneer — still starts the same way.

We're a small, family-run kitchen in Kolkata, cooking recipes that took three generations to get right and about ten minutes to fall in love with.

💬 good to know

Frequently asked

Do you take walk-ins, or should I book ahead?+
Walk-ins are always welcome, but weekends fill up fast — a table held in advance means no waiting at the door.
Is there a vegetarian menu?+
Yes — around a third of our menu is fully vegetarian, cooked on separate stations, including the Palak Paneer and a rotating vegetable curry of the day.
Can you handle nut or dairy allergies?+
Tell your server when you order. Most curries can be adapted, and we're happy to walk you through what's in each dish.
Do you deliver or is it dine-in only?+
We're dine-in and takeaway. Call ahead and your order will be packed and ready when you arrive.
Is parking available nearby?+
Street parking is available on Elgin Road, with a paid parking lot two minutes' walk from our door.
