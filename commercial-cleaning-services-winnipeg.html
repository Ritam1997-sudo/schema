Commercial Sanitization & Disinfection

After COVID, many businesses introduced disinfecting procedures that eventually became inconsistent. Now most commercial clients expect cleaning vendors to understand disinfectant dwell times, touchpoint sanitation, cross-contamination prevention, product safety, and material compatibility.

Disinfection work depends heavily on proper product use. Stronger chemicals are not always better. Incorrect disinfectant use damages surfaces, creates odor issues, and sometimes leaves residue employees complain about.

Frequent touchpoints include: elevator buttons, handrails, reception counters, washroom fixtures, door handles, and shared desks.

Questions About Commercial Cleaning Services Winnipeg

What's the difference between contract cleaning and one-time cleaning? +
A one-time clean fixes the visible mess once; a recurring commercial cleaning Winnipeg contract keeps the building at a steady standard all year. Contract cleaning means the same trained crew visits on a fixed schedule, issues are caught early, and pricing stays predictable. Most Winnipeg businesses that start with a one-time deep clean move to a recurring contract within a few months.
How much do commercial cleaning services Winnipeg cost? +
Pricing depends on square footage, flooring type, visit frequency, washroom count, occupancy, and specialized needs like floor care or carpet extraction. Most CS7 contracts are structured as monthly contracts, weekly service plans, or custom site agreements. Contact us for a site-specific estimate.
How often should my Winnipeg business be cleaned? +
It depends on traffic and business type. Offices commonly use nightly janitorial, while warehouses often run weekly schedules plus monthly deep cleaning. Many Winnipeg commercial cleaning schedules increase frequency between November and March because of salt, slush, and winter floor damage.
Are your commercial cleaners insured and trained? +
Yes. CS7 is fully insured for commercial and industrial environments, and every crew member completes WHMIS and workplace-safety training before being assigned to a site. Healthcare and clinic environments follow stricter disinfection and contamination-control protocols.
Do you bring your own supplies and equipment? +
Yes. CS7 supplies commercial-grade equipment, cleaning chemicals, and consumables. Larger facilities may require ride-on scrubbers, industrial vacuums, degreasers, and high-dust tools — standard equipment is not adequate for large industrial floors. Ask about our industrial cleaning program when we assess your site.
Can you clean after hours or around my operating schedule? +
Yes. Many office cleaning services run after business hours, retail stores clean before opening, and warehouses use split schedules around forklift operations. We build every recurring cleaning plan around your occupancy, not the other way around.
How do I choose the right commercial cleaning company Winnipeg business? +
Check four things: consistent staffing (not rotating strangers), insurance coverage, documented inspection processes, and direct supervisor communication. Our guide on choosing a commercial cleaning company in Winnipeg walks through exactly what to look for before signing a contract.
