💬 good to know

Frequently asked

Do you take walk-ins, or should I book ahead?+
Walk-ins are always welcome at both branches, but weekends fill up fast — a table held in advance means no waiting at the door.
Is the whole menu Indian, or do you really do Chinese and Continental too?+
All three, properly. Our wok section runs alongside the tandoor and grill, so a table can order Indian, Chinese and Continental together without a second kitchen involved.
Is there a vegetarian menu?+
Yes — a full vegetarian line runs across all three cuisines, cooked on separate stations, including the Palak Paneer and a rotating vegetable wok dish.
Can you handle nut or dairy allergies?+
Tell your server when you order. Most dishes can be adapted, and we're happy to walk you through what's in each one.
Do you deliver or is it dine-in only?+
Both branches offer dine-in and takeaway. Call ahead and your order will be packed and ready when you arrive.
